# Source anchor verification: Ibn Ḥajar’s twelve ranks

**Checked:** 2026-11-16
**Purpose:** verify the introductory rank definitions and example cues as a source index for the candidate-vocabulary inventory. This note does not classify corpus phrases or assign ranks from keywords.

## Edition and source hierarchy

The local rank inventory follows a searchable Dorar.net text copy identified as based on Muḥammad ʿAwwāma’s Dār al-Rashīd 1406 edition. Shamela’s official [book record](https://shamela.ws/book/8609) identifies the same editor, publisher, and 1406 edition; it also says the text source is Dorar.net and that differences from the Abū al-Ashbāl edition are placed in brackets. The repository’s [source comparison note](../SHAMELA_ALIFTA_EDITION_AND_COVERAGE_CHECK.md) records that the local page extraction begins at printed page 75 and omits the opening rank introduction.

Alifta’s official [rank-introduction page](https://sunna.alifta.gov.sa/BookToc/ViewServicePage?bookId=51&mainId=506655) displays all twelve categories and examples. Alifta’s official [book record](https://sunna.alifta.gov.sa/Book/Details?bookId=51) identifies its text as the Abū al-Ashbāl Ṣaghīr Aḥmad Shāghif al-Bākistānī edition, Dār al-ʿĀṣimah, 1413 AH. Therefore Alifta verifies the structure and supplies a comparator, but it is not the wording authority for the local ʿAwwāma 1406 text.

The repository’s edition comparison records at least two differences: rank 5 has `تغير بأخرة` in the Dorar/ʿAwwāma text and `تغير بآخره` on Alifta; rank 10 has `بقدح` versus `بقادح`. The searchable Dorar text is not a scan, and the printed 1406 pages have not been visually checked here. Preserve both readings and their source labels.

## Verified introductory anchors

The Alifta page presents this sequence; the local source inventory transcribes the ʿAwwāma/Dorar reading and paraphrases the definitions. The examples are source anchors for candidate discovery, not a claim that every observed Taqrīb wording is listed here.

| Rank | Exact example cues shown in the introduction | Definition condition to retain |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `الصحابة` | Companion status; keep separate from ordinary reliability grades. |
| 2 | `أوثق الناس`; `ثقة ثقة`; `ثقة حافظ` | Superlative, repetition, or praise combined in meaning. The complete construction matters. |
| 3 | `ثقة`; `متقن`; `ثبت`; `عدل` | A single commendatory attribute. |
| 4 | `صدوق`; `لا بأس به`; `ليس به بأس` | Below rank 3 in Ibn Ḥajar’s scheme. |
| 5 | `صدوق سيء الحفظ`; `صدوق يهم`; `له أوهام`; `يخطئ`; `تغير بأخرة`; `التشيع`; `القدر`; `النصب`; `الإرجاء`; `التجهم` | Preserve the full memory/error qualification. For innovation, retain type and whether the person advocates it. |
| 6 | `مقبول`; `فلين الحديث` | `مقبول` is conditional on corroboration; without it the wording indicates mild weakness. |
| 7 | `مستور`; `مجهول الحال` | More than one transmitter, but no authentication. |
| 8 | `ضعيف` | No recognized authentication plus a weakness statement. |
| 9 | `مجهول` | Only one known transmitter and no authentication. |
| 10 | `متروك`; `متروك الحديث`; `واهي الحديث`; `ساقط` | No authentication plus a defect that grounds weakness. |
| 11 | `من اتهم بالكذب` | Accusation of lying; distinguish the accusation from a direct applied label. |
| 12 | `من أطلق عليه اسم الكذب والوضع` | Lying/fabrication label applied to the narrator; distinguish from rank 11. |

## Implications for candidate vocabulary

- The explicit examples span status, praise, qualified praise, unknownness, weakness, abandonment, accusation, and lying/fabrication. A vocabulary work limited to `ثقة` would omit most of the source-defined range.
- The words above seed a corpus search. They do not authorize automatic rank assignment. Several definitions require corroboration, transmitter counts, authentication evidence, a defect, or a full qualification.
- Keep exact source wording, edition, observed variant, and surrounding construction in separate fields. Mark a phrase as an explicit introductory example, a corpus-observed candidate, context-dependent, or unresolved. Do not call the inventory exhaustive.
- Generation labels such as `من العاشرة` are not reliability rank 10; they belong to the separate generation taxonomy.
