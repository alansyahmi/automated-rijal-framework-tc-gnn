# Evaluative statement layer: source-context pilot v0.1

**Status:** AI-assisted review set prepared for one reviewer. It is a small codebook usability pilot, not a blind annotation study, an inter-reviewer agreement study, or a rank-labeling exercise.

## What is in the workbook

The [review workbook](../../../../outputs/evaluative_statement_layer_pilot_v0_1_2026-11-16/REVIEW_SET.xlsx) contains 16 complete entries copied from the local Taqrīb pages file. The cases cover:

- Rank 1 status cues: بدري and أم المؤمنين.
- Direct and combined praise, including a temporal/retention qualification.
- Named accusations, an ambiguous construction, plural attribution, and consensus wording.
- Superlative praise.
- Attributed and direct unknownness wording, including an unclear pronoun referent.
- A specific hadith-transmission criticism and a qualified positive assessment.

The AI suggestions are visible so the reviewer can assess whether the draft categories fit the complete text. The reviewer fields begin blank. The sheet offers three choices: **Looks right**, **Change**, and **Unsure**. If choosing Change, write the correction in the adjacent column; use the note column to explain uncertainty or missing context.

## Scope and limits

This review checks whether the statement-family, domain, claim-content, claim-mode, wording-force, and consensus-wording descriptors fit the passage. It does not assign or alter any of the twelve canonical rank labels, verify the historical truth of a reported judgement, or establish a real-world consensus. It is a single-reviewer, visible-suggestion review and cannot support an independence or inter-reviewer reliability claim.

The Arabic text is copied verbatim from the local digital extraction at data/1_raw/Shamela4_Full_DB/26__التراجم-والطبقات/3076__تقريب-التهذيب/pages.jsonl. Each row gives the local printed-page locator and Taqrīb entry number. This pilot does not compare the transcription with a printed page image.
