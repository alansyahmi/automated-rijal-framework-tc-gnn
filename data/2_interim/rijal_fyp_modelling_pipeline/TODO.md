## Decisions to preserve

- The target reference is Ibn Ḥajar’s original twelve categories; do not invent a different rank system.
- Under the user-approved operational convention, the target candidate is the first complete grade-bearing phrase in *Taqrīb*, even when attributed to an earlier critic. Preserve that attribution; do not report the convention as proof of Ibn Ḥajar’s personal agreement.
- Keep attached qualifiers and potentially rank-relevant notes in a separate context layer. Fine-grained labels for affiliations such as naṣb and tashayyuʿ are not separate targets; preserve their exact source wording and document the limitation.
- Rank 1 is Companion status and must be distinguished from ordinary reliability-grade interpretation.
- *Taqrīb* is a label source for Task A and a prohibited feature source for Task B.
- Exact lexical matches suggest candidate labels; they do not establish historical attribution, provenance, target eligibility, or independent corroboration.
- Current candidate rules remain unenabled unless separately reviewed and approved through the existing registry process.
- Earlier schemas, annotation freezes, and forensic outputs are not to be overwritten by this project folder.
- Preserve the original reviewer’s fine-grained labels as a parallel annotation layer when adding broad parent roles. For sequence labels such as `nisba_1` and `nisba_2`, retain the distinction when the source spans support it.
