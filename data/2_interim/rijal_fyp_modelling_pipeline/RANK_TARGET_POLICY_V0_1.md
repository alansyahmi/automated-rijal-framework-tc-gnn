# Rank Target Policy v0.1

**Status:** project policy for candidate construction; do not treat as a historical finding or an enabled automatic rule.

## Target convention

For this project, the target is the rank of the **first complete grade-bearing phrase in the Taqrīb entry**. Apply this convention even when the phrase explicitly attributes the assessment to an earlier critic—for example, wording such as `صدوق عند النسائي`. Under the project convention, that first phrase supplies the target candidate.

This is an operational assumption made to define a consistent FYP target. It does not independently prove that Ibn Ḥajar personally formulated, adopted, or agreed with every attributed assessment. Preserve any named evaluator or attribution wording in the source text and in separate attribution metadata; do not erase it to make the convention appear historically established.

## Boundaries

- Select the first **complete grade-bearing phrase**, not the first matching substring. Preserve attached qualifications that form part of that phrase, such as an error or late-change qualifier. Do not cut a compound at its first word.
- Later, separate biographical statements, transmission symbols, and affiliation descriptions are not separate supervised rank targets under this policy.
- Detailed affiliation labels such as *naṣb* and *tashayyuʿ* are outside the annotation target and will not receive separate ultra-granular labels. Preserve their wording in the source text; do not normalize, delete, or use it to invent a new rank.
- Continue to use Ibn Ḥajar's original twelve-rank taxonomy. Do not create, merge, or renumber ranks.
- This target-selection convention does not remove conditions in the rank definitions. Where a rank requires corroboration, transmitter counts, authentication history, a defect, or another fact not established by the entry, keep the rank candidate conditional or unresolved until that evidence is available.
- If the first grade-bearing phrase cannot be separated from its qualification or attribution confidently, abstain and record why.

## Recommended data fields and reporting language

Keep the exact first grade-bearing phrase, its rank candidate, the full entry locator, and any explicit attribution together. For reporting, call the target the **Taqrīb first-grade rank under the project convention** or a **first-grade proxy target**. Do not describe it as a verified record of Ibn Ḥajar's personal agreement unless separate evidence establishes that claim.

Affiliation text can remain visible as source context without being converted into a separate model label. Its exclusion is a declared research limitation, not evidence that the affiliation is irrelevant to classical rijāl scholarship.

## Review status

This policy is distinct from the frozen boundary annotations. It guides the next phrase-inventory and shadow-review stages. Candidate matches remain unassigned until the complete phrase boundary, rank definition, and any required conditions have been checked.
