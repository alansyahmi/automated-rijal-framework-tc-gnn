## Safeguards applied

- Rank 1 remains Companion status, separate from reliability. “Rؤية”, disputed/reportive companionship, battle participation, lineage, and honorifics do not automatically establish it.
- Rank 2 requires the source-defined superlative, repetition, or combined-praise construction. Rank 5 memory/error terms retain full qualifications; innovation cues stay conditional on type and advocacy context.
- Rank 6 `مقبول` requires corroboration. Ranks 7 and 9 remain distinct by transmitter count; Ranks 8 and 10 retain authentication/defect conditions.
- Rank 11 accusation of lying is distinct from Rank 12 an applied lying/fabrication label. Attributed `كذبه`, `كذبوه`, and `متهم` constructions need source-level distinction.
- Generation wording such as `من العاشرة` is not reliability Rank 10. External critic appraisals do not become Ibn Hajar’s rank through lexical similarity.
- No rank here establishes authorship, historical provenance, transmission route, or an actual case-level narrator grade.
