## 2. Taqrīb taxonomy source

> الخامسة: من قصر عن الرابعة قليلا، وعليه تدور عبارة: صدوق سيء الحفظ، أو صدوق يهم، أو له أوهام، أو يخطئ، أو تغير بأخرة، أو اختلط في الآخر.

The fifth-rank definition explicitly includes `أو يخطئ`. S01 combines the base grade `صدوق` with the qualification `يخطىء`, which corresponds to that rule with an orthographic spelling variant. The exact Taqrīb spelling is preserved in the statement row. The original dataset spelling `يخطئ.` and the Taqrīb spelling `يخطىء` are both preserved; candidate-field correspondence is `minor_variation` because of hamza placement and terminal punctuation.

The rank rule was checked in the local text transcription and edition metadata. The printed page image was not independently inspected.

## 4. Identity resolution

The identity match is confirmed using the matching lineage, al-Jari sobriquet, Banu Nawfal patronage, and Hijazi identification. Al-Mizzi's *Tahdhīb al-Kamāl*, vol. 31, pp. 522-524, entry 6913, records `يحيى بن محمد بن عبد الله بن مهران الجاري`, `مولى بني نوفل بن أسد`, and `حجازي`. It explains that al-Jar is a port. No additional geographic precision is inferred.

The correspondence between Taqrīb and al-Mizzi supports the identity decision, but may reflect literary compilation dependence. It is not treated as independent attestation, and the grade is excluded from identity resolution.

## 5. Historical investigation: reports preserved by al-Mizzi

The local transcription of al-Mizzi's entry reports several earlier assessments: al-Bāhilī described him as `ثقة`; al-Bukhārī's report says `يتكلمون فيه`; Ibn Ḥibbān's entry says `يغرب`; an Abu ʿAwānah report through ʿAbbās al-Dūrī and Yaḥyā al-Zimmī calls him `ثقة`; and Ibn ʿAdī is reported as saying `ليس بحديثه بأس`.

These are reports preserved in al-Mizzi's compilation. The cited earlier works and transmission routes were not independently inspected for this adjudication. The record therefore does not establish the primary wording, route, or independence of each report.

The earlier assessments show a varied biographical record. Some reports describe the narrator as trustworthy or acceptable, while others record criticism or unusual narrations. They provide historical context for Ibn Hajar's later assessment, `صدوق يخطىء`, but the precise relationship between each earlier report and Ibn Hajar's formulation has not been established. In particular, `يغرب` and `يتكلمون فيه` are not treated as direct equivalents of `يخطئ`. The former does not by itself establish retention errors; the latter reports criticism collectively without identifying its critics or grounds.

## 6. Research implication

Rank 5 represents a qualitative category below Rank 4, characterized by qualifications such as weak retention, occasional error, or later-life deterioration. It should not be interpreted as a uniform numerical interval on a scalar reliability scale. This observation does not make every qualification in Rank 5 identical in implication.
