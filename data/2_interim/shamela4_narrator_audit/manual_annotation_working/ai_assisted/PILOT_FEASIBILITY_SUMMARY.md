# Shamela4 20-case pilot: provisional feasibility summary

**Status:** Provisional AI-assisted working result, recorded 2026-11-11. The user accepted the AI draft for current use and reported no corrections to the AI-suggested Pass 2 sheet. This is not independent expert validation.

## What was reviewed

The purposive pilot contains 20 tuples across all six sampling strata. The AI-assisted Pass 1 draft proposes 43 claim rows. The separate AI-assisted Pass 2 draft compares those proposed claim subjects with the attached narrator metadata. The original tuple text and v2.5 templates remain unchanged.

The user previously reported matching the name, ID, and passage to Shamela records for PILOT01–PILOT03. That report concerns those three cases only; it does not verify the full pilot or the semantics of Shamela's integer source ID.

## Feasibility reading

The 20 examples were enough to produce a provisional, multi-claim annotation and surface cases where the relationship between a passage and the attached narrator remains uncertain. The 23 unresolved Pass 2 suggestions show that identity reconciliation needs context or reviewer judgment for many claims. This is a useful feasibility signal, not a failure rate: the labels were generated by AI and the sample is purposive.

No annotation-time measurements or systematic protocol-difficulty log were recorded, so this pilot cannot yet establish human throughput or codebook usability. There is one available human reviewer, so inter-reviewer agreement cannot be calculated. The user's lack of corrections to the AI Pass 2 draft is not an independent-review agreement statistic.

## Conclusions and limits

- The package can support further human checking of claim segmentation and claim-to-attached-narrator relationships.
- It does not establish historical speaker attribution, quotation accuracy, narrator reliability, or the number of independent historical evaluations.
- It does not establish that source IDs denote quotations, pages, passages, works, or bibliographic records.
- Documentary provenance remains a separate unresolved task; tuple text and the AI draft alone are insufficient evidence to resolve the upstream source-ID entity.
- No original field was corrected, swapped, normalized, or used for graph construction or model training.

## Recommended next research step

Before scaling annotation or using these labels as research targets, have a second Arabic-trained reviewer independently annotate the same cases from the blinded text, then reconcile disagreements. If a second reviewer is not available, keep this pilot explicitly AI-assisted and single-reviewer, and focus the next manual work on obtaining documentary evidence for source-ID provenance rather than claiming annotation reliability.
